## 5. Accelerators: findings and recommendations

### 5.1 Is TF past the accelerator stage?
- **Incubation**: yes, TF doesn't need it for desks or mentoring. But incubators (KIIT-TBI, FTBI NIT Rourkela, IIMCIP) are the gateways to SAMRIDH, GENESIS and NIDHI money. Join one once the Odisha Pvt Ltd exists.
- **Cash accelerators**: TF is on time. YC takes idea to early revenue. Peak XV Surge's FAQ says "no minimum traction". Accel Atoms wants companies that raised under $2M. speedrun and PearX target pre-seed.
- **Talent programs**: TF is past them. Entrepreneur First is for individuals without a team, South Park Commons for people exploring ideas, Antler India's residency for pre-launch teams.
- **Too early for**: Upekkha (~$100K ARR, no cash), Google for Startups Accelerator India (seed to Series A), T-Hub T-Angel (Rs 3 to 5L MRR), Mastercard Start Path (seed raised), Prime, Stellaris, Info Edge ($2M+ or Series A).
- **Cheque size mismatch**: YC ($500K), Surge ($500K to $5M) and Atoms (up to $1M or $2M) would more than fill the round. A yes changes the size and shape of the raise. Only 100X.VC (Rs 1.25 Cr) and funds like 100Unicorns, WEH and Huddle ($200K to $600K) match.
- **Traction alone won't get TF in.** 3 restaurants is proof of life, not market fit. Show week-on-week growth, repeat-order rates and money flowing through Razorpay.

## 7. How to get DPIIT recognition

Written from general knowledge, not a fresh check of the portal. Confirm on startupindia.gov.in.

### 7.1 Eligibility
- Private Limited company, LLP or registered partnership.
- Under 10 years from incorporation.
- Turnover under Rs 100 Cr in every financial year.
- Working on innovation or a scalable business model.
- Not formed by splitting or restructuring an existing business. Build TF as a fresh company, not a carve-out of MSM Global, and describe it that way.

### 7.2 Steps (free, online)
1. Incorporate Table Foundry Pvt Ltd on the MCA portal (SPICe+). Get Certificate of Incorporation, PAN and TAN together. About 1 to 2 weeks.
2. Register on startupindia.gov.in and create a startup profile.
3. Fill the DPIIT recognition form: CIN, incorporation date, Bhubaneswar address; founder and director details; industry IT Services / Software, sector SaaS or AI; stage "Early traction"; short answers on problem, solution, innovation, scalability and jobs.
4. Upload: Certificate of Incorporation; pitch deck, website or product video (show the WhatsApp ordering flow and AI campaigns); optional IP, awards or funding letters.
5. Self-declaration that you meet the criteria.
6. Certificate with DIPP number, usually in 2 to 10 working days. Answer any clarification quickly.

### 7.3 Avoiding rejection
- Be specific: "AI agent that runs repeat-order and loyalty campaigns for independent restaurants over WhatsApp, with direct ordering and payments via Razorpay Route". Not "restaurant marketing services".
- Don't describe TF as an agency or consulting business.
- Keep name and address identical across MCA and DPIIT.

### 7.4 After recognition
- Startup Odisha recognition with the same company.
- AWS Activate $5K, Zoho, Freshworks credits.
- 80-IAC tax holiday (separate Inter-Ministerial Board approval) and angel tax exemption (matters for the pre-seed).
- GENESIS and SAMRIDH via KIIT-TBI, Kotak BizLabs Season 4, Credit Guarantee Scheme.

### 7.5 Timeline
About 3 to 4 weeks from starting incorporation to DPIIT in hand. Most of it is incorporation.
